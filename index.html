IN/AT-SYNC

INterior/ATmosphere SurveY of sub-Neptunes in Chains — a JWST Cycle 5 Large Program using NIRISS/SOSS and NIRSpec/G395H to measure atmospheric mean molecular weight, C/O, and envelope mass fraction for nine sub-Neptunes in four resonant chains, spanning ages from 75 Myr to 6 Gyr.

PI Rafael Luque (IAA-CSIC) Program JWST GO 10047 Time 196.5h Status Implementation

Across the Galaxy, planetary systems play to a different tune than our own. Most Sun-like stars host compact sub-Neptunes locked in resonant chains at birth — but the harmony rarely lasts: most chains turn dissonant within the first billion years. The few survivors are pristine laboratories, their architectures frozen at disk dispersal and their atmospheres shielded from the impacts that reshape most systems. IN/AT-SYNC observes four such survivors — TOI-6109, TOI-1136, HD 110067, and TOI-178 — spanning 75 Myr to 6 Gyr. Combined with resonant chains already observed by JWST in earlier cycles, this builds the first age-resolved view of sub-Neptune origin and nature.

Data Products

Following the precedent set by KRONOS, all intermediate and final data products will be uploaded to Zenodo, and each paper gets its own numbered repository under the in-at-sync GitHub organization. A shared repository enforcing pipeline-output uniformity across independent reductions is planned. Documentation on data formats will be posted here as it's created.
